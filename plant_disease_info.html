Plant Disease Information & Cure Guide

Rose Plant

Disease: Black Spot

Symptoms: Dark round spots with yellow ring on leaves.

Cure: Prune affected leaves, apply neem oil spray weekly.

Tulsi (Holy Basil)

Disease: Downy Mildew

Symptoms: Grey mold under leaves, leaf curling.

Cure: Improve air flow, use fungicidal spray.

Mango Tree

Disease: Anthracnose

Symptoms: Black lesions on flowers and fruits.

Cure: Use copper-based fungicide, prune infected parts.

Lemon Plant

Disease: Citrus Canker

Symptoms: Yellow halo spots on leaves and fruits.

Cure: Apply copper spray, remove infected areas.

Neem Plant

Disease: Powdery Mildew

Symptoms: White powder on leaves, stunted growth.

Cure: Apply sulfur-based spray, water at base.

Snake Plant

Disease: Root Rot

Symptoms: Leaves yellowing, roots soft and black.

Cure: Remove rotten roots, replant in dry soil.

Peace Lily

Disease: Leaf Spot

Symptoms: Brown dry spots on leaf surface.

Cure: Remove affected leaves, avoid water on foliage.

Money Plant

Disease: Stem Rot

Symptoms: Blackened stems, leaf wilting.

Cure: Reduce watering, cut off affected parts.

Spider Plant

Disease: Tip Burn

Symptoms: Leaf tips turn brown.

Cure: Use filtered water, reduce fertilizer.

Aloe Vera

Disease: Soft Rot

Symptoms: Mushy base, foul smell.

Cure: Remove infected parts, dry and replant.
